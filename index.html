About This Guide

Helping Grade 10 students explore their next educational step.

🎯

Purpose of the Website

The SHS Career Guide is designed to provide accessible information about Senior High School pathways and possible career opportunities. It aims to help Grade 10 students explore their interests and make informed educational choices.

🧭

How to Use the Guide

Browse the pathway descriptions, complete the interest assessment, search possible careers, and use the information as a starting point for conversations with trusted adults and school personnel.

Website Features

The guide includes a dashboard, pathway information, an interest assessment, a searchable career list, and dark mode.
